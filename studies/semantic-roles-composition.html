TYPE IS A SYSTEM OF DECISIONS

One language.
Five distinct jobs.

Typeface is a choice. Meaning is the rule. Different kinds of information should not all speak with the same visual voice.

Semantic roles

Five roles, one common alignment system. No decorative numbering or competing cards.

displayShort brand statement
heading-1 / 2 / 3Document and section hierarchy
bodyContinuous reading
label / captionNavigation, controls, explanatory notes
dataEngineering values and identifiers

TWO RULES / ONE SHARED GEOMETRY

Choose the
role first.

AI should identify the content’s job before assigning numerical styling. Display may be used for short marketing headlines; long product titles normally stay in Sans. Heading size alone does not determine HTML heading structure.

Chinese and
font fallback.

IBM Plex Sans SC is the preferred Chinese candidate, but glyph coverage and production assets are not verified. Use the documented fallback: IBM Plex Sans SC → PingFang SC → Microsoft YaHei → sans-serif. Font fallback must not silently alter content or hierarchy.
