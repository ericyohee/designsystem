03 / PRINCIPLES & IMPLEMENTATION

Frozen principle

Choose the role first.

AI should identify the content’s job before assigning numerical styling. Display may be used for short marketing headlines; long product titles normally stay in Sans. Heading size alone does not determine HTML heading structure.

Implementation / Under review

Chinese and font fallback.

IBM Plex Sans SC is the preferred Chinese candidate, but glyph coverage and production assets are not verified. Use the documented fallback: IBM Plex Sans SC → PingFang SC → Microsoft YaHei → sans-serif. Font fallback must not silently alter content or hierarchy.
