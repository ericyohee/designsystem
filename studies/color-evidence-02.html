01 / ENGINEERING QUESTION

Can the production process consistently remain within these limits?

Actual capability, variation and measurement reliability have not been assessed here. This band represents the design specification—not measured output, distribution, process capability or yield.

01 / The source of the graphic

24.50 mm ± 0.05 mm yields a lower allowable limit of 24.45 mm and upper allowable limit of 24.55 mm. The band connects the range endpoints and marks the nominal midpoint. It is not a histogram or an empirical capability chart.

02 / The color decision

The open warm-white reading surface remains continuous. One deep violet strip has a precise function: it denotes the permitted range. The brand is recognizable without an unrelated dark card or a traffic-light status badge. The color must never be the only carrier of meaning; values, labels and explanatory text remain present.

03 / The design method

Inspired by ABCD's concept-led identity work and the EUC project's proportional systems, but this tolerance composition is original to the Geniotek study—not an ABCD published template. IBM's semantic separation remains relevant: technical representation, link/action, brand identity and warning/error are different roles, even if some share color families.

04 / Agent instructions

Use a visual range only for a verified relationship. Calculate the endpoints from the supplied nominal and tolerance; show units and an accessible textual summary. If the source provides no measured samples, do not imply a statistical distribution. Retain the approved grid/typography logic and inspect the mobile three-value grouping before promoting this layout to a production pattern.
